What Private browsing does

  • Deletes your browsing info, search history, and form data when you close all private windows
  • Saves cookies and temporary site data in an isolated partition that is completely discarded on exit
  • Blocks third-party trackers, telemetry beacons, and fingerprinting scripts automatically

What Private browsing doesn't do

  • Hide your browsing from your school, employer, or internet service provider
  • Hide your IP address or physical network location from the websites you visit
  • Keep you signed in to your web accounts after you close this window
