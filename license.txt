PART II — PRIVACY POLICY & DATA PRACTICES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

SECTION 7 — PRIVACY FIRST COMMITMENT
──────────────────────────────────────
7.1  Core Philosophy. Ocal Browser is designed with a foundational "Privacy
     First" philosophy. We believe your browsing activity is personal and
     private. We do not build advertising profiles, sell your data, or transmit
     your browsing history to our servers.

7.2  Local Data Storage. The following categories of data are stored exclusively
     on your local device and encrypted at rest:
     - Browsing history and session data
     - Bookmarks and reading lists
     - Saved passwords and credentials (via built-in Vault)
     - Form autofill data
     - Browser preferences and settings
     - Extension data

7.3  Ocal Sync (Optional). Ocal Sync is an opt-in, zero-cloud, LAN-based
     synchronisation feature. When enabled:
     - All sync data travels exclusively over your local area network (LAN)
     - Data is encrypted end-to-end using the displayed PIN/QR code
     - No sync data is routed through our servers or any cloud provider
     - You may disable or unpair sync at any time via Settings > Sync

7.4  AI Copilot. When you use the built-in AI Copilot feature:
     - Queries are processed by the configured AI provider
     - Conversation context is session-isolated and not persisted beyond
       your browsing session unless you explicitly save it
     - No browsing history is automatically sent to AI providers

7.5  Telemetry & Crash Reports. We may collect anonymised, aggregated telemetry
     and crash reports to improve Software quality. This data:
     - Contains no personally identifiable information (PII)
     - Contains no browsing history, URLs, or page content
     - May be disabled at any time via Settings > Privacy

7.6  Update Checks. The Software periodically contacts our update servers to
     check for newer versions. This request includes only the current Software
     version number and your operating system version. No personal data is
     transmitted.

7.7  Children's Privacy. The Software is not directed at children under the age
     of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We
     do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
