PART II — PRIVACY POLICY & DATA SECURITY ARCHITECTURE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

SECTION 7 — FOUNDATIONAL PRIVACY COMMITMENT

7.1 Zero-Cloud & Local-First Philosophy. Ocal Browser was engineered from the ground up on an uncompromising local-first architectural paradigm. We do not collect, profile, sell, rent, monetize, or harvest your web browsing records, search queries, URL visits, form submissions, or reading history.

7.2 Zero Commercial Advertising Profiling. We have no advertising network integrations. No tracking identifiers, device fingerprints, or advertising beacons are generated or broadcast by the application.


SECTION 8 — LOCAL VAULT & ON-DISK ENCRYPTION

8.1 Cryptographic Isolation. All sensitive browsing artifacts — including saved login credentials, security tokens, autofill entries, and personal bookmarks — are persisted exclusively inside the local Ocal Vault on your storage drive.

8.2 Cryptographic Standard. Vault contents are encrypted at rest utilizing industry-grade AES-256-GCM cryptography integrated with the Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI). Encryption keys are bound to your local Windows user account; no keys are ever transmitted off your physical hardware.


SECTION 9 — OCAL CONNECT PEER-TO-PEER LAN SYNC PROTOCOL

9.1 Peer-to-Peer Subnet Architecture. When enabled, Ocal Connect facilitates cross-device synchronization (e.g. between desktop and mobile) through direct, local area network (LAN) socket connections. No sync data ever traverses our servers or third-party cloud infrastructure.

9.2 Ephemeral Pairing Cryptography. Pairing is authenticated through dynamic QR/PIN handshakes with Diffie-Hellman key exchange. Data packets are end-to-end encrypted across your Wi-Fi or Ethernet subnet. Sync can be unlinked at any time with a single click in Settings > Sync.


SECTION 10 — AI COPILOT & DOCUMENT INTELLIGENCE

10.1 User-Initiated Execution. Built-in AI assistance runs strictly upon explicit invocation by the user (such as clicking the AI Studio button or pressing the copilot shortcut). The browser never silently scrapes your web tabs in the background.

10.2 Context Isolation & Zero Model Training. Conversations and document analysis sessions are session-isolated in volatile memory. User queries are not retained to train foundation models, nor are they associated with persistent browsing personas.


SECTION 11 — WEBSHIELD ANTI-TRACKER & PERFORMANCE FILTERS

11.1 On-Device Rule Evaluation. Ocal WebShield employs optimized network filters directly in the browser process to intercept cross-site tracking cookies, fingerprinting scripts, cryptominers, and deceptive advertisements before they load.

11.2 Zero External Filtering Telemetry. Blocked request statistics are aggregated solely inside local memory to display your session dashboard. No URL block reports are transmitted to external auditing servers.


SECTION 12 — ANONYMOUS TELEMETRY & USER CONTROLS

12.1 Diagnostics Scope. To maintain platform reliability across hardware architectures, minimal anonymized crash traces and CPU load indicators may be gathered. These payloads contain zero personal identifying information (PII), no URLs, no IP addresses, and no page contents.

12.2 Unrestricted Opt-Out. You maintain sovereign control over telemetry. You can disable all crash reports and diagnostic signals at any time under Settings > Privacy & Security.
